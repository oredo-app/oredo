Oredo The app for clients

What you need, brought to your door.

Get deliveries from any shop, book a cleaning, call a plumber, and more, all from one app. The services you see depend on what is available in your area.

  • Save your places on the map once, reuse them every time
  • Several services in one app
  • Compare offers by price and rating
  • Chat in the app or call your pro directly
  • Pay in cash, no card needed
  • Rate every order to keep quality high

How to use Oredo

  1. Sign up with your phone

    Verify your number with a one-time SMS code and complete your profile in under a minute.

  2. Add your place

    Pin your address on the map and pick your area, Shebin El Qanater or Benha, so pros know exactly where to go.

  3. Create your order

    Choose the service, describe what you need, add your notes, and set the price you want to pay.

  4. Pick the offer you like

    Pros nearby accept your price or propose theirs. Compare names, ratings and prices, then accept one.

  5. Follow it live

    Chat with your pro or call them, and watch your order move from accepted to in progress to done.

  6. Pay and rate

    Pay the agreed price in cash, plus the cost of any items bought for you, then rate your pro.

Oredo Pro The app for service providers

Your skill. Your hours. Your price.

Oredo Pro brings you requests from clients in the areas you choose, for the service you provide. Accept their price or send your own, and get paid in cash for every job.

  • Work in Shebin El Qanater, Benha, or both
  • No shifts: go online whenever it suits you
  • Accept the client's price or propose yours
  • Payment goes straight to you, in cash
  • Track your completed orders and earnings
  • Build a rating that wins you more orders

How to start with Oredo Pro

  1. Sign up with your phone

    Download Oredo Pro, verify your number and complete your profile with a clear photo of yourself.

  2. Choose your service and regions

    Pick the service you provide and the areas you want to receive orders from.

  3. Upload your documents

    Your national ID (front and back) and criminal record certificate. For vehicle services, add your vehicle licence and plate number.

  4. Get approved

    Your documents are reviewed, and you get a notification as soon as your account is approved.

  5. Send your offers

    New orders near you arrive as notifications. Accept the client's price or propose your own.

  6. Do the job and get paid

    Do the work with care, keep any receipts, and collect your cash from the client.
