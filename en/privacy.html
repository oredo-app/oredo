1Who this policy covers

  • Clients — people who use the Oredo app to request services (such as delivery) from service providers.
  • Pros — independent service providers who use the Oredo Pro app to receive and carry out requests.

One person can have one client account and one pro account. Each account is separate, and its data is handled as described below.

4Who can see your data

The other party of an active order

To make an order possible, the client and the pro handling the same order can see each other's:

  • name, profile photo and rating;
  • phone number, so they can call each other about the order;
  • the order details, including the pickup location and address.

Pros can never see clients' saved places that are not part of their order. Clients can never see pros' verification documents.

The operator

Only the operator, or a person the operator authorises to help run the apps, can access data, and only when needed: reviewing pro documents, answering support requests, or investigating a reported problem.

Service providers that process data for us

We use Google Firebase (Google LLC) to host and run the apps: sign-in, database, file storage, notifications, crash reporting, analytics and app integrity. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own security and privacy commitments, and may store it on servers outside Egypt.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your personal data with advertisers, marketers or any other third party for their own purposes.
  • We only disclose data to authorities when Egyptian law requires it, and only the data the request legally covers.

5How we protect your data

  • All data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (TLS).
  • Data is encrypted at rest on Google Cloud servers.
  • Strict access rules on the database and file storage: each account can only read and change its own private data, and verification documents are not readable by other users.
  • Sign-in requires verifying your phone number with a one-time code.
  • Only one device session is active per account; signing in on a new device ends the old session.
  • App integrity checks block requests that do not come from the genuine app.
  • Sensitive values stored on your phone are kept in encrypted storage.
  • Access by the operator, or anyone the operator authorises, is limited to what each task needs.

No system is 100% secure. If we become aware of a breach that affects your data, we will notify you and the competent authorities as the law requires.

6How long we keep data, and account deletion

While your account is active

We keep your data while your account is active, because it is needed to provide the service.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Account → Delete account in the app. Deletion is not possible while you have an open order; finish or cancel it first.

When you delete an account, the following is deleted automatically:

  • your profile name, phone number and profile photo, which are removed from your public profile;
  • all photos and files you uploaded (profile photo, ID photos, criminal record certificate, vehicle licence);
  • your saved places (clients) or your verification record and pending offers (pros);
  • your notifications and your Support chat;
  • your device tokens, so you stop receiving notifications.

If you have no other active Oredo account (client or pro), your sign-in identity is also deleted: phone number, email, gender and date of birth. Your phone number and email become free to register again.

What remains after deletion

Records of past orders, the messages in those order chats, and ratings stay in our system. They are needed for the other party's history, for accounting, and for handling disputes. These records are no longer linked to your name, photo or phone number; you appear as "Deleted user". The text of messages you sent in order chats stays visible to the other party of that order.

Crash reports and analytics are kept by Google Firebase in aggregate form for the retention period set in those services. They do not include your name, phone or documents.

7Your rights

Under Egyptian law you may:

  • access your data — most of it is visible in your account;
  • correct your data — edit your profile in the app, or ask Support;
  • delete your data — delete your account in the app (Section 6);
  • withdraw consent — turn off notifications or location in the app or your phone settings, or delete your account;
  • object to or ask about any processing — contact Support.

We answer requests within a reasonable time and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

8Age requirement

The apps are only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will show the new version in the app, and when the changes are significant we will ask you to accept them again before you continue using the app. The "Effective date" at the top shows the latest version.
